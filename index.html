OUR SERVICES

Y.M.A Multiventures Concept specializes in the design and management of robust domestic logistics networks. We build the framework for B2B and B2C operations, allowing you to drive your business forward by focusing on the growth and sales of your goods.

We construct customized storage and freight networks, providing cost certainty and a quicker route to market. We eliminate the discovery phase and help increase your local exposure by offering a single point of contact for all your logistics needs.
